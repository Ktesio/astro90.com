# Navigation island artwork

These four original illustrations were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on September 14, 2026. They are decorative navigation artwork, not gameplay screenshots. The PNG masters preserve the generated transparency. The website uses 900 px and 480 px WebP deliveries, resized without cropping or changing the artwork.

Masters: `lighthouse.png`, `inkube.png`, `about.png` and `contact.png`, all 1536 × 1024 RGBA. Their web versions are in `static/media/navigation/`, named `{island}-480.webp` and `{island}-900.webp`. Delivery settings: Sharp resize to the named width, WebP quality 82, alpha quality 95, effort 6. The game screenshots remain separate assets.

`data/navigation-shores.json` holds convex shorelines sampled from the PNG alpha (threshold 200, every fourth pixel). Regenerate them with `python3 scripts/export_navigation_shores.py` after changing an island illustration. The route planner scales these outlines to the rendered artwork and adds room for the hull, hover lift and turns.

## lighthouse

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one transparent-background island illustration for the interactive navigation of Astro90, a mobile games studio. Primary request: a beautifully painted miniature island viewed from above at a 45 degree aerial angle, isolated on a genuinely transparent background, with only a thin fringe of pale foam and teal shallow water close to the shore. Cohesive art direction: sophisticated hand-painted gouache and digital matte painting, tactile stone and pine textures, windswept northern coast at blue hour, navy shadows, muted slate-blue rocks, deep pine foliage, warm saffron yellow lights #F4C84C. Rich atmospheric and material detail, plausible architecture. It must feel like a small discoverable world, not an icon, not a 3D app tile, not a cartoon emoji, no low-poly facets. Composition: landscape 3:2, entire island visible, island occupies central 80% of canvas with at least 8% clear transparent margin all around. Readable silhouette, lit roofs and stone edges against the dark sea where it will be composited. No sky, no horizon, no background rectangle, no extended surrounding ocean, no text, no letters, no symbols, no UI or watermark. Light falls from upper left.
Subject: a craggy crescent island, pale slender lighthouse on its highest rock slightly left of center, a warm glowing lantern but NO long painted light beam, a little keeper cottage with amber windows, stone steps curling down toward a short wooden landing at lower right, tiny coastal pines and coastal boulders. A clear tall lighthouse silhouette. The island is substantial and gently asymmetrical.
