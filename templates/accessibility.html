Using this
website.

Choose how things move

Use the motion control above, in the navigation, or in the footer. Reduced motion stops the boats, lighthouse beams and scroll effects. We remember your choice for this browsing session.

If your device asks websites to reduce motion, we follow that setting. You can change it in your device’s accessibility settings.

Navigate with a keyboard

Press Tab to move between links and buttons, or Shift + Tab to go back. The first link skips to the main content. Choose Explore to open the island map. Each game, About and Contact has a direct link. Focusing an island highlights it, just like hovering.

Press Esc to close the navigation or screenshot viewer. Focus returns to the control you opened it from.

Take a closer look

Select a game screenshot to open a larger view. The viewer includes previous and next controls, and you can use the left and right arrow keys to move between screens. Browser zoom also works throughout the site.

If an image doesn’t load, use “Try again.” Project descriptions and links remain available. With JavaScript disabled, screenshot links open the image directly and the main navigation stays visible.

Tell us about a problem
